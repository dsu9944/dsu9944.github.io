👨‍👩‍👦 For grown-ups

This game practises quick addition and subtraction facts from the NSW Mathematics K–10 syllabus (additive relations), going from combinations to 10 up to harder next-stage work: facts to 20, bridging through ten with teen and two-digit numbers, adding and taking away tens, missing numbers on either side of the equals sign, fact families, and doubles and halves to 100. Levels 7 and 14 are timed sprints with a bonus for quick answers. Getting it right counts more than being fast.

Challenge levels: each level runs at a challenge level from 1 to 5. Higher levels use bigger numbers, ask for typed answers instead of multiple choice, hide the ten-frame pictures until a hint is used, and give a shorter speed-bonus time. A score of 9 or 10 moves that level up one challenge step for the next round. A score of 4 or less moves it back down, but never below the setting you choose here.

Difficulty:

Easy starts at challenge level 1 (like the original game, with pictures and multiple choice). Normal starts at 2. Challenge starts at 3. The 🔈 button reads each question aloud. Hints still help, but give 5 points instead of 10 (3 points from challenge level 4). Progress is saved on this device only.
