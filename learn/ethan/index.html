👨‍👩‍👦 For grown-ups

This game practises quick addition and subtraction facts from the NSW Mathematics K–10 syllabus (Stage 1, additive relations): combinations to 10, doubles, near doubles, making ten to add (bridging) and subtracting by thinking addition. It starts with ten-frame pictures and moves to doing it in your head. The last level is a speed round with a bonus for quick answers. Getting it right counts more than being fast.

The 🔈 button reads each question aloud. Hints are free to use but give 5 points instead of 10. Progress is saved on this device only.
