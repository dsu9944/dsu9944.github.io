👨‍👩‍👦 For grown-ups

This game covers data from the NSW Mathematics K–10 syllabus. Levels 1–8: sorting objects into groups, tally marks, picture graphs where one picture stands for one thing, column graphs (most, least, how many more, altogether), and pie charts with halves and quarters. Levels 9–16 stretch into harder next-stage work: picture graphs where one picture stands for 2 or 10 (with half pictures), column graphs with scales counting by 2s, 5s and 10s and unlabelled gridlines, two-step questions, two-way tables, pie charts in eighths and comparing two pies, spotting what's wrong with a graph, and choosing a true conclusion from the data.

Challenge levels: each level runs at a challenge level from 1 to 5. Higher levels use bigger numbers, more categories, typed answers instead of multiple choice, scales and two-step questions. A score of 9 or 10 moves that level up one challenge step for the next round. A score of 4 or less moves it back down, but never below the setting you choose here.

Difficulty:

Easy starts at challenge level 1 (like the first version: multiple choice, small numbers). Normal starts at 2. Challenge starts at 3. Every question is read aloud when it appears (you can turn this off below), and the 🔈 button repeats it. The feedback shows the answer on the graph. Hints give 5 points instead of 10 (3 points from challenge level 4). Progress is saved on this device only.
