👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Covers adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator (NSW Mathematics K–10), starting with fraction bars and pizzas, then number-line jumps in the parallel number line style used in class. It moves on to missing numbers, a gentle first look at totals bigger than one whole (5/4 = 1 1/4), and word problems. Levels 9–12 stretch further: related denominators (1/2 + 3/8, 2/3 − 1/6), adding and subtracting mixed numbers (with regrouping), and two-step word problems. A common mistake is adding the bottom numbers (2/5 + 1/5 = 3/10). The game spots it and shows on parallel number lines why it gives a different amount.

Denominators: 2–12 at Easy, growing to 15, 16, 20 and 24 at higher Challenge levels. At Challenge level 3 and above, the pictures for Take Away, Missing Parts and Beyond One Whole stay hidden until a hint is used, and Pizza Plus asks for a typed answer. An answer that is equivalent (e.g. 1/2 instead of 2/4) is accepted. Hints are free but give 5 points instead of 10 (3 at Challenge level 4 and 5). Progress is saved on this device only.

Difficulty:

Challenge (the default) starts each level at Challenge level 3: bigger numbers, fewer pictures and typed answers. Easy is the original version of each level. Getting 9 or 10 right steps that level up (to Challenge level 5); 4 or fewer steps it back down.

The answer key prints on its own page.
