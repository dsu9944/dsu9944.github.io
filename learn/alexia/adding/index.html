👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Covers adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator (NSW Mathematics K–10, Stage 2), starting with fraction bars and pizzas, then number-line jumps in the parallel number line style used in class. It moves on to missing numbers, a gentle first look at totals bigger than one whole (5/4 = 1 1/4), and word problems. A common mistake is adding the bottom numbers (2/5 + 1/5 = 3/10). The game spots it and shows on parallel number lines why it gives a different amount.

Denominators: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12. An answer that is equivalent (e.g. 1/2 instead of 2/4) is accepted. Hints are free but give 5 points instead of 10. Progress is saved on this device only.

The answer key prints on its own page.
