Equivalent fractions are different names for the same amount. Like 1/2 of a pizza is the same as 2/4 or 4/8 of it! Finish a level with 6 or more out of 10 to unlock the next one. New: simplifying, improper fractions and comparing in Levels 8–11! Score 9 or 10 and the level gets harder. ⚡

👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Levels follow the NSW Mathematics K–10 syllabus for fractions: picture models first (fraction walls and pizzas), then parallel number lines (the method used in class this term), then choosing equivalents, missing numbers (multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same number) and placing fractions on a number line. Levels 8–11 stretch further: simplest form with denominators up to 100, improper fractions and mixed numbers (including their equivalents), and comparing and ordering unlike fractions by making the denominators the same. At higher Challenge levels the counting labels, number-line labels and pictures are hidden until a hint is used, denominators go up to 24 on the pictures and up to 100 elsewhere, and Match It! asks for every equivalent fraction.

Wrong answers show a picture explaining why. Hints are free to use but give 5 points instead of 10 (3 at Challenge level 4 and 5). Progress is saved on this device only.

Difficulty:

Challenge (the default) starts each level at Challenge level 3: bigger numbers, fewer pictures and typed answers. Easy is the original version of each level. Getting 9 or 10 right steps that level up (to Challenge level 5); 4 or fewer steps it back down.

The answer key prints on its own page.
