👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Covers decimals to two places (NSW Mathematics K–10, Stage 2). It starts with tenths and hundredths linked to fractions (3/10 = 0.3, 47/100 = 0.47) using bars and 100-grids. Then she places and reads decimals on number lines from 0 to 1, zooms from tenths into hundredths, and goes beyond 1 (1.7 = 1 7/10). After that she compares and orders decimals and finishes with money and measurement problems.

The number lines follow the parallel number line style used in class for fractions, with a fraction line lined up above a decimal line and a dashed red box showing they are the same spot. The game looks out for common mistakes. One is thinking a longer decimal is bigger (0.25 > 0.3), which it explains with 100-grids and parallel tenths/hundredths lines. Others are writing 7 hundredths as 0.7 instead of 0.07, swapping the tenths and hundredths digits, and leaving out the whole number. Hints are free but give 5 points instead of 10. Progress is saved on this device only.
