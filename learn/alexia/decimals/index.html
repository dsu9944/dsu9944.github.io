👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Covers decimals (NSW Mathematics K–10). It starts with tenths and hundredths linked to fractions (3/10 = 0.3, 47/100 = 0.47) using bars and 100-grids. Then she places and reads decimals on number lines from 0 to 1, zooms from tenths into hundredths, and goes beyond 1 (1.7 = 1 7/10). After that she compares and orders decimals and finishes with money and measurement problems. Levels 10–15 stretch further: thousandths, number lines beyond 3 (such as 6.4 to 7.4 and 3.4 to 3.5), ordering mixed sets of fractions and decimals, adding and subtracting money (including change), and rounding to the nearest whole number, tenth or hundredth. At Challenge level 3 and above, the picture questions (bars and 100-grids) and most multiple-choice questions are swapped for typed answers, and Zoom In! uses numbers bigger than 1.

The number lines follow the parallel number line style used in class for fractions, with a fraction line lined up above a decimal line and a dashed red box showing they are the same spot. The game looks out for common mistakes. One is thinking a longer decimal is bigger (0.25 > 0.3), which it explains with 100-grids and parallel tenths/hundredths lines. Others are writing 7 hundredths as 0.7 instead of 0.07, swapping the tenths and hundredths digits, and leaving out the whole number. Hints are free but give 5 points instead of 10 (3 at Challenge level 4 and 5). Progress is saved on this device only.

Difficulty:

Challenge (the default) starts each level at Challenge level 3: bigger numbers, fewer pictures and typed answers. Easy is the original version of each level. Getting 9 or 10 right steps that level up (to Challenge level 5); 4 or fewer steps it back down.
